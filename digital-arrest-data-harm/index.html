This section documents organized cyber fraud, data theft, and digital coercion affecting ordinary citizens in India. Material focuses on victim experience, evidence preservation, and the institutional gaps in victim protection and recovery. Documentation includes victim reports, law enforcement filings, published investigations, and analysis of how State systems can be strengthened to prevent and respond to digital crime.
