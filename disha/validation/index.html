DISHA Validation and Source Verification Basis

The published 97% figure should be read as an internal documentary source-matching figure within the reviewed case-file set. It does not mean that a court, ministry, regulator or outside institution has issued a 97% certification for DISHA.

What the 97% figure means

DISHA reviewed factual claims included in the published case files and matched them against identified government records, court documents, audit material, official correspondence and other primary public records.

In the current reviewed set, 97% of the assessed case-file claims were supported by the cited official documentary record. This is a statement about documentary source matching inside the reviewed set, not a statement that every constitutional conclusion has been externally adjudicated.

After factual verification, DISHA examines whether institutions, duties, timelines and administrative responses align with constitutional accountability. That second-stage Article 12 conclusion remains DISHA's analysis unless a court or public authority separately records the same conclusion.

Two-stage reading rule

Stage 1: what the official records establish

Facts directly supported by government documents, court records, audit reports, official replies or other identified primary records.

Stage 2: what DISHA identifies

After factual verification, DISHA connects those records to institutional duties, timelines and Article 12 accountability. That constitutional conclusion is analytical unless independently recorded by a court or authority.

Public claim-ledger categories

The public claim ledger uses the following categories so readers can separate direct records, corroborated material, DISHA analysis and unresolved matters.

Official record

Directly supported by identified government, court, audit or official documentary records.

Corroborated

Supported by a combination of public records, reporting or cross-source documentation, but not disclosed here as a single official record entry.

DISHA analysis

Analytical findings made by DISHA after connecting verified records across institutions, duties and timelines.

Unresolved

Questions requiring further response, investigation, adjudication or documentary clarification.

Public ledger snapshot

The current public claim ledger discloses 27 rows: 13 official-record entries, 5 corroborated entries, 6 DISHA analytical entries, and 3 unresolved items.

Methodological limits

  • The 97% figure reflects internal documentary review, not independent third-party certification.
  • The public claim ledger is a disclosure layer for readers, not proof of the internal reviewed denominator by itself.
  • The reviewer is the inventor of DISHA, so every published claim should remain open to independent scrutiny against the linked source record.
  • The measure concerns reviewed factual claims in the case-file set. Constitutional accountability conclusions remain analytical unless separately recorded by a court or authority.
