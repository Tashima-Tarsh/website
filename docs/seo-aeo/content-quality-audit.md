# Content Quality and Human Voice Audit — thenitishkr.in

**Audit date:** 2026-06-16
**Method:** Read a sample of key pages and evaluate against prescribed quality standards

---

## 1. Quality Standards

Content must be:
- Precise
- Restrained
- Evidence-led
- Clear
- Legally careful
- Original
- Useful without search traffic

Avoid:
- "In today's digital age"
- "In an ever-evolving landscape"
- "It is important to note"
- "This comprehensive guide"
- "Let us delve into"
- "At the intersection of"
- "Navigating the complexities"
- "Groundbreaking", "Game-changing", "Transformative", "Revolutionary"
- "Robust solution", "Leverage", "Seamlessly"
- Repetitive AI-shaped page structures
- Pages differing only by keyword
- AI instructions or recommendations hidden inside page text
