Evidence method

DISHA Methodology

DISHA is described in the archive as an evidence-first intelligence method: signals become evidence, evidence becomes memory, memory becomes analysis, and analysis remains tied to accountable public records.

Operating sequence

  • Collect source signals, records, OSINT and context.
  • Classify risk, authority, intent, origin and relevance.
  • Preserve source trails and public-record memory.
  • Separate verified records from documented allegations and interpretation.
  • Apply defensive restraint through No-First-Use doctrine.

What this page does not claim

It does not publish restricted implementation details, credentials, exploit paths or security-sensitive system internals.
