Human Review and Remedy in Digital Systems

A serious digital decision requires more than a generated result. The citizen must be able to understand, challenge and correct the record through an accountable human path.

Minimum remedy architecture

  • notice of the decision and its practical effect;
  • access to the relevant record or reason;
  • correction where the record is wrong;
  • human review by an identified authority;
  • preservation of evidence for appeal or audit.

Why Article 12 matters

Human review is not a customer-service preference when public power is involved. It is the route through which responsibility becomes visible and remedy becomes possible.

Limits

This page is a framework note. It does not assert that any particular institution has denied review without a source record.
