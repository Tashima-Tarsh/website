Privacy Policy

thenitishkr.in is a privacy-first, non-commercial public-interest research archive. It does not sell products, services, user data, visitor data, research access, or personal information.

01

Privacy-first operation

thenitishkr.in publishes research, public records, media references, feeds, and entity information. It is designed as a public-interest record, not as a commercial data-collection platform.

02

No sale of data

The website does not sell, rent, trade, broker, or commercially exploit visitor data. It does not operate as advertising software, a surveillance business, or a consumer data product.

03

Technical data

Standard hosting, security, CDN, browser request metadata, and crawler logs may be processed by infrastructure providers to operate, secure, cache, and protect the website.

04

Contact requests

If you contact the site through email, linked platforms, or the connect page, the information you provide is used only to respond, maintain necessary records, protect legal or research documentation, and handle public-interest communication.

05

External services

When you open Substack, Medium, LinkedIn, X, Instagram, Facebook, Quora, GitHub, Google News, or media outlet pages, those third-party services may apply their own privacy policies.

06

Cookies and analytics

The public website is intended to operate with minimal tracking. Necessary technical cookies or security mechanisms may be used for site operation, security, or availability.

Privacy requests and record corrections.

Requests are reviewed against the published record, applicable privacy interests, and the archive's documentary responsibilities.
