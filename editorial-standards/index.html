Editorial standards

Editorial Standards and Contact

thenitishkr.in is a non-commercial public-interest research archive. It separates verified records, documented allegations, research analysis, public commentary, and adjudicated findings so readers can evaluate each item in its proper legal and factual context.

Read standardsContact
Trust protocol

Records first. Interpretation second.

Public claims are framed with source status, legal-safe language, and visible correction routes.

Verified recordsDocuments and official source paths remain visible where available.
AllegationsClaims are marked as allegations until adjudicated or independently verified.
AnalysisResearch interpretation is kept separate from records.
CorrectionsGood-faith correction requests are reviewed.
01

Source classification

Pages may include verified records, documented allegations, research analysis, public commentary, media references, and adjudicated findings. These categories should not be read as the same evidentiary status.

02

Legal-safe wording

Accused persons, alleged conduct, pending matters, and contested claims are described with careful language. This archive does not convert allegations into findings.

03

Correction route

Requests for correction, verification, attribution, privacy, or takedown review may be sent to nitish@thenitishkr.in. Include the page URL and the specific passage.

04

Verification standard

The authenticity of a source document and the conclusion drawn from that document are evaluated separately. The archive identifies what the document says, what factual proposition it supports, what contradiction appears across records, what DISHA assesses, and what remains for an authority or court to determine.

05

Document citation standard

Consequential factual statements should identify, where available, the issuing authority, document title, reference number, date, relevant page or paragraph, and a direct source link.

06

Corrections and version history

Material corrections, documentary responses and significant page updates are recorded with a date and a short description of the change.

04

Non-commercial status

The website is maintained as a non-commercial public-interest project. It is not for sale and is not operated as a product funnel or advertising business.

05

Entity clarity

This site refers to Nitish Kumar (thenitishkr), researcher, author, National Cyber Security Scholar, and inventor of DISHA. He is not the politician of the same name.
