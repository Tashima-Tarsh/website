# Phase 1 Editorial Content Review

Branch: `seo/phase-1-site-architecture`  
Review status: Draft for owner review. Do not merge without editorial approval.

This document lists material new or substantially reorganized copy for the Phase 1 information architecture work. The goal is to let the owner review writing without hunting through every HTML file.

## Editorial principles applied

- Existing strong copy was preserved where it already served the intended purpose.
- New child pages are derived from existing published repository material, live-page content, and the evidence inventory.
- No new legal finding, court outcome, statistic, allegation, quote or institutional statement was invented.
- Framework language is labelled as research, analysis or proposal where the source material does not establish settled law.
- Digital-arrest and data-harm pages distinguish public record, documented allegation, source document, and unresolved question.

## Claims deliberately avoided

- No claim that Digital Constitutional Personhood is settled Indian law.
- No claim that DISHA has a measured accuracy rate, because no transparent denominator, method and sample were present in the material reviewed.
- No claim that any documented allegation is an adjudicated finding.
- No claim that digital-arrest source documents establish final criminal liability.
- No claim that every evidence inventory item has complete issuing-authority/date metadata; the existing inventory explicitly marks several items for manual verification.
