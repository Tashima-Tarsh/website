Evidence Methodology

Case structure

Each case file is structured by responsible authority, negligence issue, constitutional link, fund or cost issue, evidence status, source documents, unanswered questions, and official record trail. Source links may include PDFs, RTI records, CPGRAMS entries, PMO grievance records, letters, emails, screenshots, audit notes, and financial references.

National Audit Layer

Money and authority

The national audit layer follows tax allocation, money received, money sanctioned, money used, money unexplained, and the authority that signed, certified, closed, ignored, or benefited from the record. This connects public-money accountability with Article 12 bodies and source-linked documentation.

Source Archive

Source-linked record

The source archive will hold PDFs, RTI replies, CPGRAMS records, PMO grievance records, letters, emails, screenshots, audit records, financial trails, and case-wise evidence documents. Each case page must include a View Source area that can be populated later.
