Origin record

DISHA Origin Record

The DISHA origin record is presented through two distinct source points: a foundation sketch dated 25 April 2012 and a later 2013 Bihar Council of Science & Technology correspondence record. The page does not treat either record as a commercial certification.

What the record establishes

  • the archive identifies a 2012 hand-made foundation sketch;
  • the DISHA page links that sketch to the evidence, memory and accountability architecture;
  • the evidence inventory lists a 2013 Bihar Council of Science & Technology record as institutional correspondence.

What remains limited

The origin record supports chronology and research context. It does not, by itself, prove every present capability, deployment or validation claim.
