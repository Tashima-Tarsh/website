Eight Findings Listed in the DISHA 6.6 Disclosure

The disclosure does not rest on assertion. It rests on documented, verifiable findings that DISHA 6.6's policy analysis has produced - findings that no Indian agency has acted upon despite the evidence being either publicly available or formally submitted through official channels.

012,000+ NBFCs under Chinese infiltration

The MCA and RBI regulatory apparatus permitted over two thousand Non-Banking Financial Companies to operate as conduits for Chinese-linked economic extraction and covert capital movement inside India's financial system.

0214 Chinese nationals released without prosecution

Fourteen individuals who entered India, compromised critical digital infrastructure, and caused demonstrable national security harm were allowed to return to China - by the ED and CBI. No charge. No trial. No public explanation.

03Chen Wei - C2 Server King

Chen Wei was arrested by the US DOJ in Cambodia, 2026. His command and control infrastructure was used in coordinated attacks across Asian digital systems with direct, documented links to India.

04SilverPush and InMobi

Both Indian AdTech platforms are formally recorded at the US Federal Trade Commission for illegal cross-device surveillance and consumer data exploitation. India's regulatory bodies have taken no substantive domestic action.

05NDMA Empty Vault

Policy analysis returned an empty vault result under NDMA framework evaluation. NDMA Acts are non-operational. Disaster preparedness certifications are potentially false. Sanctioned national security buffers have not been deployed.

06The money was followed. The data was abandoned.

The CBI and ED, in their handling of financial crime investigations of this period, made a choice that investigators and legal scholars have since identified as a foundational error - they followed the money and left the data. Traditional enforcement doctrine tracks currency movement, property attachment, and financial trails. What it consistently missed - and what the Tech Dollar Virus Scam exposed with precision - is that in a digitally infiltrated crime, the data infrastructure is the crime. The money is merely its exhaust. By prioritising attachment proceedings over digital forensics, both agencies closed cases at the financial layer while the command and control architecture beneath it - the servers, the access pathways, the coordinating network - remained untouched and operational. The most visible consequence of this investigative gap is documented in intelligence records referenced at thenitishkr.in and cited before the Supreme Court: fourteen Chinese nationals who had physically entered India, compromised critical digital infrastructure, and whose presence was recorded - were allowed to return to their country without charge, without trial, and without their data ever being forensically extracted. The money was followed. The data was abandoned. The operators walked out. The connected public record is preserved at W.P. (Crl.) No. 163/2026 and the MeitY digital-governance file.

07Systematic examination leaks

The same compromised digital infrastructure that enabled the infiltrations above is the root cause of examination leaks that have denied merit-based opportunity to millions of Indian students.

084x fund inflation by elected representatives

Infrastructure works billed to taxpayers at four times actual cost by current Members of Parliament and Legislative Assemblies. Public money designated for citizens - redirected. Documented. Unaddressed.
