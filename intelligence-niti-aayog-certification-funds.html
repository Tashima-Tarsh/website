Constitutional issue

Where Article 12 becomes practical.

Whether certification, dashboard reporting, and fund movement can be treated as accountability when field records and citizen outcomes remain contested.

The case belongs to the DISHA intelligence archive because the central question is not only what happened, but which public authority can be asked to explain, verify, correct, or remedy it.

Evidence questions

Questions the record must answer.

  1. Which authority signed or relied on the certification?
  2. Which fund head, scheme, or public money trail is connected?
  3. Which record proves verification rather than only reporting?
  4. Which citizen outcome contradicts the certification claim?
