Public-interest research on digital governance and constitutional accountability.

Read the Archive Start with Article 12

This archive documents thirteen evidence case files built on public records, Supreme Court petitions, RTI trails, and source-linked data. The research centres on a single question: when every authority reports success, every dashboard reports progress, and every certification reports compliance — why do citizens continue reporting the same failures? The work spans Article 12 constitutional accountability, the DISHA Intelligence Architecture, Digital Constitutional Personhood, and W.P.(Crl.) No. 163/2026 before the Supreme Court of India.

Evidence architecture for a constitutional question

The archive is organised around the search for accountability.

When a citizen is harmed — by digital arrest, biometric failure, data theft, or administrative neglect — can the State still find them? This archive documents the research, records, and petitions built in pursuit of that constitutional question.

Article 12 Infected

Article 12 Infected describes how public authorities, schemes, dashboards, certifications, and grievance systems can grow while citizens struggle to locate accountability, remedy, and constitutional responsibility.

Framework

Digital Constitutional Personhood

The framework asserting that a citizen's digital identity, data, and online presence are an extension of their constitutional personhood under Articles 14, 19, and 21 of the Constitution of India.
