## Mapping Methodology

Each major page is mapped to:
- **Primary reader question** — What brings someone to search for this page?
- **Primary search phrase** — Most likely keyword phrase
- **Secondary phrases** — Related questions
- **Entity terms** — Important concepts for schema
- **Search intent** — Informational, navigational, or transactional
- **Evidence available** — What repository supports answers
- **Desired action** — What should reader do next?
