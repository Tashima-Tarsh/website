# News UTM Distribution Links

Purpose: use these links only when sharing articles outside the website. Do not place UTM parameters in canonical tags, sitemap URLs, internal navigation, or article-to-article links.

Campaign naming rule:

- `utm_medium=social` for LinkedIn and X.
- `utm_medium=newsletter` for Substack.
- `utm_medium=syndication` for Medium.
- `utm_medium=private_share` for WhatsApp and Telegram.
- `utm_campaign` uses the article slug in snake_case.
