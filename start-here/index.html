Reader rule

Record first. Assessment second.

Professional reading starts by separating what is documented from what is argued, and what is unresolved from what has already been decided.

  • Official records are not the same as allegations.
  • Petitions and representations are submissions, not judgments.
  • Analytical conclusions should be checked against source documents.

Reading discipline

How to read this archive properly.

Official facts should be traced to court records, government documents, RTI replies, CAG audit material, administrative correspondence or authenticated public records. The archive links each factual claim to its primary source document wherever the record is published.

Petitions, representations and grievances are documented submissions. They are not final findings unless an authority or court expressly records such a finding. For example, W.P.(Crl.) 163/2026 was disposed on 19 May 2026 with a direction to MeitY; that direction is procedural, not a ruling on the underlying factual claims.

This page should help a first-time reader move quickly from orientation to source verification.

Step 01

Confirm identity and scope

Verify the author, distinguish the archive from unrelated names, and understand whether a claim is about DISHA, a case file, a petition, or a constitutional framework.

Step 02

Open the source-linked case page

Move from this overview to the relevant case page, then read the source register, court material, government record, or RTI reply before citing the analysis.

Step 03

Cite the document, not only the summary

Where a primary source is published, cite the exact document. Where a matter remains unresolved, describe it as unresolved rather than overstating the record.

For journalists

What this archive is and what it is not.

What this archive is

  • A public-interest documentary record maintained by Nitish Kumar (thenitishkr)
  • An evidence-first research archive linking claims to primary source documents
  • A petition and grievance record connected to Supreme Court filings and government correspondence
  • A structured intelligence case archive covering NDMA, MeitY, NITI Aayog, ODF, and SIR
  • A media-verified record with coverage from PTI, Economic Times, The Tribune, Deccan Herald, Verdictum, ABP Live, New Indian Express, and LawTrend

What this archive is not

  • Not a court judgment - petitions filed are documented submissions, not adjudicated rulings
  • Not a commercial product - non-commercial public-interest project
  • Not the Nitish Kumar Bihar politician - distinct researcher-author identity
  • Not anonymised - researcher identity is publicly verified via ORCID, Wikidata, and institutional records
  • Not a replacement for primary documents; source records should be checked before publication.
